# Language of the lineup

- **Neighbor**: one of the 24 named faces shared by both players.
- **Secret**: the neighbor a player keeps concealed for the match.
- **Archivist**: the computer opponent.
- **Clue**: a truthful yes/no answer to a visible-trait question.
- **Candidate**: a neighbor consistent with a player's earned clues.
- **Flipped card**: a personal elimination mark, reversible without spending a turn.
- **Final guess**: naming a neighbor instead of asking a trait question; correct wins, incorrect loses.
- **Round**: one player question followed by one Archivist action, unless a final guess ends the match.
