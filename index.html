    WELCOME TO THE CLUB

    A little curiosity goes a long way.

    1. Keep a secret. Choose one of the 24 neighbors. The Archivist chooses independently; you may choose the same person.
    2. Take turns. You go first. Ask one yes/no trait question, then let the Archivist take a turn. Its questions about your secret are answered truthfully for you.
    3. Narrow the field. Click cards to flip them down or back up. “Apply known clues” updates your board using only answers you have earned. Flipping cards costs no turn.
    4. Make a final guess. Instead of asking, select a neighbor and confirm. Correct wins; incorrect loses. One card left still needs a final guess.

    Visible traits: dark, copper, gold or silver hair; hat, glasses, scarf, earrings and freckles. Card descriptions provide the same clues as the illustrations.

    Tab moves between controls; Enter or Space activates buttons. Arrow keys move between cards. Escape cancels a guess or closes a dialog. Touch any control to use it. Refresh starts a fresh game; switching tabs preserves your current game.
